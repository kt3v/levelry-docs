---
description: Use when the primary deliverable is a stakeholder-ready Final Concept document for a game or game feature, covering its objective, gameplay, product and metric goals, functional requirements, UI/UX, monetization, success criteria, and analytics events without implementation details. Do not select for mechanics design or balancing, level layouts, or engineering task creation.
---

A final concept document must follow this structure:

1. **Objective** — what this product/feature is and why it exists.
2. **Goals** — gameplay, product, and metric goals.
3. **Functional Requirements** — what must work, without implementation details.
4. **UI/UX** — how it looks and feels.
5. **Monetization** — where and why the money comes from, if applicable.
6. **Expected Outcome** — what result counts as success.
7. **Analytics Events** — which events we track and why.
