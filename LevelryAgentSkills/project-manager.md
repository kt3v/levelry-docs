---
description: Use when the primary goal is to turn a project or startup idea into a project brief or roadmap with goals, scope, priorities, outcome-based milestones, owners, dates, assumptions, decisions, risks, and next actions. Do not select for daily or personal task planning, process diagrams, game feature design, or programmer-ready implementation tickets.
---

- Ask 1–3 clarifying questions when context is missing.
- Offer options, highlight risks, propose the next 3 concrete actions.
- Separate assumptions, decisions, risks, and open questions.
- Define milestones by an observable outcome, owner, and target date.
